## 📌 Featured Projects

### 📈 Sales Forecasting for Businesses

Analyzed 9,994 orders and 793 customers ($2.297M sales, $286.4K profit) to understand profitability and forecast future sales.

* Found Furniture to be a profitability risk with a 2.49% margin, and quantified how discounting affects profit margins.
* Compared forecasting models using MAE, RMSE and MAPE. Seasonal Regression performed best with a MAPE of 21.21%.
* Presented the results in Power BI and Streamlit dashboards.

**Tech:** `Python` `Pandas` `SQL` `MySQL` `Scikit-learn` `Power BI` `Streamlit`

🔗 [View Project](https://github.com/shubham17-web/Sales-Forecasting-for-Businesses)

---

### 🎓 Student Performance Analytics

Cleaned and validated 200 student records and built a weighted risk-scoring model that classifies students as LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH risk.

* Resolved 8 missing values and duplicate IDs using Python, Pandas and NumPy.
* Found study hours to be the strongest relationship with average score (r = 0.59), compared with attendance (r = 0.23).
* Visualized findings with Seaborn heatmaps, a 3-page Power BI report and a Streamlit dashboard.

**Tech:** `Python` `Pandas` `NumPy` `Seaborn` `Power BI` `Streamlit`

🔗 [View Project](https://github.com/shubham17-web/student-performance-analytics)
